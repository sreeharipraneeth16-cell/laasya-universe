Some things shouldn't be typed. But here we are.

Laasya,

I've tried to write this so it sounds calm. It doesn't. So here it is, plain: you are my favourite part of every day.

Not the big cinematic parts — the small ones. The text I wait for. The story you tell twice because I asked. The way you say my name when you're tired.

I don't want to impress you with a website. I want to be the person who makes your ordinary days easier, for as long as you'll let me.

So this is me, asking. With stars, because apparently subtlety left the chat. 💫
